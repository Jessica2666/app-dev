# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror drama television series created by the *Duffer Brothers*. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, the story follows a group of young friends as they uncover secret government experiments and terrifying supernatural forces.

## Why I Love It
* **Nostalgic 80s Vibe:** Great soundtrack, retro fashion, and classic pop culture references.
* **Compelling Characters:** Strong friendships and character development across every season.
* **Mystery & Suspense:** Keeps you guessing with plot twists and an intriguing alternate dimension called the *Upside Down*.
